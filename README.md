## 👋 About Harisl

Harisl Ltd. is an Egyptian software studio founded in **2018** and headquartered in **Nasr City, Cairo**. We design, engineer and grow digital products for businesses across **Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Bahrain** — with clients reaching into Europe and Asia.

We started in Giza, expanded across the Gulf, and today we build the systems real businesses run on every day: cash registers that never miss a sale, telecom platforms, online stores, and practical AI that saves time and money — not buzzwords.

**Founder & CEO:** Eng. Mohamed Samir
